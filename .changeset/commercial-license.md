Relicense from MIT to PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 plus a paid commercial license.

Noncommercial use — hobby projects, learning, research, education, charities and
government institutions — stays free under
[PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).

Commercial and business use now requires a paid license: **$99 a year per company**,
covering every `@richkitjs` package with unlimited developers and unlimited products.
There is no license key, no activation check and no telemetry — the editor still never
phones home. Every company gets 90 days of free commercial use before it has to buy, and a
lapsed subscription keeps a perpetual license to the last version it paid for.

Every package now ships `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL` and `NOTICE` in its tarball, and
`@richkitjs/core` prints a one-time licensing notice on install.

`0.1.0` was published under MIT and stays MIT — that release is unaffected.
